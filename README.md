# Retail Sales Data Visualization

## Objective
Analyze two years of retail sales to find trends, regional differences and
the most profitable product categories, so the business can plan stock and promotions.

## Tools Used
Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Google Colab

## Key Insights
- Sales grow each year and peak every December.
- East is the top region in every category; South is the lowest.
- Electronics has the highest sales but the lowest profit margin.
- Clothing has the best margin and should be favoured in promotions.

and push high-margin categories instead of only high-sales ones.
